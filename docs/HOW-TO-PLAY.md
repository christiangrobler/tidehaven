# How to play

Tidehaven is a race to the winning number of victory points on an island laid out at random. Collect resources from the dice, spend them on roads, settlements and cities, trade with other players and the bank, and buy development cards.

## On the controller

- **Your hand** (resource and development cards) is at the bottom. Nobody else sees it.
- **Mini map**: drag and pinch to move around. Tap **Road**, **Settle** or **City**, then tap a glowing spot on the map.
- **Roll dice**: press the button on your phone and the dice roll on the TV.
- **Trade**: bank trades, or offers to another player. Their controller shows the offer to accept or decline.
- The lightbulb gives a hint. The round arrow re-centers the map.

## Turn outline

1. Roll the dice. Every settlement or city touching a tile with that number yields that tile's resource.
2. Trade if you like.
3. Build or buy development cards.
4. End your turn.

When the robber number is rolled, the roller moves the robber and steals a card, and players holding too many cards discard half.

## Setup

Players take turns placing two starting settlements with roads (the second round in reverse order). Your second settlement pays out the resources next to it.

## Boards and variants

The host chooses the board, expansions and options before pressing **Start**. Two-player scenarios are available; every normal rule still applies.

## Single device

Open `game.html` (hot-seat, or against the computer) if you do not have a TV.
